# Introduction

Greetings and welcome to the world of biopunk and flesh-magic. Biomancy is a flesh-based tech mod that aims to provide
a unique (magi-)tech mod experience that includes themes of manipulating living things and creating living/organic constructs/machines.
Most things are loosely inspired by biological processes/quirks while still retaining creative freedom by explaining "plot holes" with "magic".

## Biomancy Has Integrations for:

- [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei)
- [Pehkui](https://modrinth.com/mod/pehkui)
- [KubeJS]($technical/kubejs)

## Biomancy Has Compatibility for:

- [Digester](@biomancy:digester) accepts any food automatically
- Raw Meat from: Farmer's Delight, Alex's Mobs
- [Alex's Mobs](https://modrinth.com/mod/alexs-mobs)
- [Alex's Caves](https://modrinth.com/mod/alexs-caves)

## Compatibility between Biomancy and [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create)

The compatibility for [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create) has been extracted into its own dedicated mod called Bio-Factory.

Features:

- Nutrient Fluid for transporting nutrients over pipes into Biomancy Machinery
- mixing recipe for converting Nutrient Paste into Nutrient Fluid
- heated compacting recipe for converting Nutrient Fluid to Nutrient Bar
- compacting recipe for Nutrient Bars
- compacting recipes for various Flesh Blocks
- acid splashing recipes (fan processing) for corroding blocks such as copper, stone bricks, etc.
- fluid effects for open-ended pipes spilling gastric acid and nutrients fluid
- ability to pump fluids (potions , honey, milk and builders tea) into the cradle
- alternative recipe for Rich Soil from Farmer's Delight
- ability to combine the Acolyte Helmet with the Goggles
- Flesh Door & Iris Door work on contraptions/trains

The mod can be found on CurseForge and Modrinth:

- https://modrinth.com/mod/biofactory
- https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/bio-factory
