# Essence Anemia

Prevents the extraction of [Mob Essence](@biomancy:essence) from affected mobs.

- affects Mobs & Players
- max level: 1
- Not curable with Milk
- Effect doesn’t disappear on Player death
- Makes the affected mob “aggressive”
