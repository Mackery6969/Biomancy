# Frenzy

- affected mobs become hostile towards anyone and perform melee attacks
- mobs without attack damage (e.g. animals) deal at least 1 point of damage
- gives stats similar to [Strength II](@minecraft:strength) + [Resistance II](@minecraft:resistance) + [Speed II](@minecraft:speed)
- applies [](@biomancy:withdrawal) Status Effect for 2 min 30 sec after the frenzy ends
