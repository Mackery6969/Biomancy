# Corrosive
Like poison, it does damage over time, unlike poison, it is far quicker, and ignores immunity frames. 
It damages worn armor more than normal damage types, and it applies [](@biomancy:armor_shred).

- deals fatal corrosive damage every 8 ticks
- deals more damage when the afflicted entity is in water
- not curable with milk
- [converts Zombies to Skeletons]($getting_started/gastric_acid)

## Corrosive Damage
- bypasses immunity frames
- doesn’t bypass armor
- deals +20% more damage to armor
