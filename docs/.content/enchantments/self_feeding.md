# Self-feeding

When any held or equipped living item is damaged, every 30 ticks (~1.5 seconds) randomly pick an item with the enchantment.
And consume fresh raw meat food or nutrient paste/bar from the players hotbar. (It picks the item that wastes the least amount of nutrients when consumed to heal the item.)

## Properties

Max Level: 1
Applicable to: Living Items
