# Bloomberry

> This weird but beautiful meat… fruit? (Is it a fruit? Or is it a meat?)
> Nevertheless! The mound tends to sprout these fleshly clam “flowers” on its exterior. For the mound, 
> they serve as a means of spreading it further, but for us? They serve as a wonderful snack if you’re quick enough.
> - Acoylte Cerinox

A unique food item that when consumed, will grant a **random** potion effect. The list of random
potion effects extends to every vanilla potion effect, as well as every modded potion effect (from the mods you have installed).

So if you happen to have a mod with a particularly deadly potion effect installed, 
it is advised to ingest the bloomberry responsibly.

- Ingredient for brewing the potion of [](@biomancy:primordial_infestation)
