# Impaler

This weapon is a living tool which runs on nutrients instead of durability.

An arm mounted heavy projectile launcher that throws bone spears that deal
heavy impact damage against armored/heavy enemies otherwise impales enemies to surfaces.

- greatly reduced movement speed
- greatly reduced melee attack speed

<PrefabObtaining />

## Operation

- `HOLD RIGHT CLICK` to charge the spike and increase its damage (like a bow)
- when fully charged it
	- shoot it out while piercing every enemy it comes across and dealing impact damage.
		- Max Charge Timer: 2.5 sec? (0.4 SPS)
		- Max Damage Bonus: 20 + 7.5% Max HP
		- Armor/Shield bypass: 50% damage or chance?
		- Disables Shields and force un-equip them or fling them away? (i.e. drop them on the ground)

## Repair

This is a living tool that consumes uses raw meat or nutrients to repair itself.
You have to feed the tool with these items while inside the inventory GUI as you would fill a bundle.
