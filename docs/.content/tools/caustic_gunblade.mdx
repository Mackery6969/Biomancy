# Ravenous Claws

This weapon is a living tool which runs on nutrients instead of durability.

It can alternate between melee and ranged mode and utilizes acid to damage its targets.
The acidic ammunition regenerates by itself after 5s of no usage.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Operation

- press the action key to toggle between melee and gun mode

## Abilities

### Acid Coat

On Right-Click coats the blade in Acid for the next two attacks.
Full-Strength Attacks inflict one stack of Armor Shred for 4s and one stack of Corrosion for 3s.
Critical Hits inflicts level 2 effects.

## Repair

This is a living tool that consumes uses raw meat or nutrients to repair itself.
You have to feed the tool with these items while inside the inventory GUI as you would fill a bundle.
