# Enlargement Serum

Induces growth in Slimes, Magma Cubes and Flesh Blobs.

## Effect on Players

Not applicable or when [Pehkui](https://modrinth.com/mod/pehkui) is installed increases size scale by 0.25 up to a maximum scale increase of 2.0

## Effect on Mobs

- increases size of Slimes or Magama Cubes by 1 step
- increases size of Flesh Blobs by 1 up to a max size of 10 (3x3x3 m)
- when [Pehkui](https://modrinth.com/mod/pehkui) is installed increases size of all mobs by scale of 0.25 up to a maximum scale increase of 2.0

## Pehkui Scale

The scale step size and max scale can be configured in the [server_configs]($developers/configs)
