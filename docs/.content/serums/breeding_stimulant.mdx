# Breeding Stimulant

Potent drug that temporarily forces animals into a state of hyper-fertility,
allowing them to reproduce repeatedly for a short time.

Use with caution, as this stimulant may lead to undesirable mutations in the offspring.

## Effect on Players

Not applicable.

## Effect on mature Animals and Villagers

- applies the [](@biomancy:libido) status effect for 14 seconds
