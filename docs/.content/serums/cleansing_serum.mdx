# Cleansing Serum

Burns away all foreign substances inside a living entity.
Very effective for sticky status effects that refuse to be healed with milk.

## Effect on Players

- causes max amount of exhaustion (40 points, essentially consumes saturation)

## Effect on Players and Mobs

- removes all [status effects](@minecraft:effect) (even incurable ones)
- removes all absorption hearts
- resets [Pehkui](https://modrinth.com/mod/pehkui) size of [](@biomancy:enlargement_serum) and [](@biomancy:shrinking_serum)

## Effect on Mobs

- cures [Zombie Villager](@minecraft:zombie_villager) into [Villager](@minecraft:villager)
- converts [Wither Skeleton](@minecraft:wither_skeleton) into [Skeleton](@minecraft:skeleton)
- removes forced age from [ageable mobs](@minecraft:breeding#Ageable)
