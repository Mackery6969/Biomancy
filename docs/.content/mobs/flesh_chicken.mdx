# Flesh Chicken
- neutral mob that alerts others when attacked
- uses a ranged acid spit attack that deals a small amount of damage and applies 4 seconds of [](@biomancy:corrosive) effect
- attacks undead mobs
- occasionally lays [](@biomancy:acidic_egg) or []($getting_started/gastric_acid) splatters
- immune to acid
- can be bred

## Obtaining
- occasional side effect of using [](@biomancy:breeding_stimulant) on chickens
