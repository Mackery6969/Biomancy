# Bio-Forge

The primary way to craft Biomancy items and blocks.

- This machine gives an alternative crafting method to the player, also allowing the crafting of lots of mod specific items.
- It mainly uses the components obtained from the decomposer for its recipes
- Requires fuel

## Operation

- Each craft uses up 1 fuel
- Fuel input slot receives (Food, Nutrients, Nutrient Paste, Nutrient Bar)
- Different craftable options are shown on the left, selecting one prepares the recipe
- Crafting works similar to modern crafting in video games, you have the requirements and their specific amounts, when requirements are met in the player’s inventory, the player
  can remove the item from the output slot to finish the craft, similar to the grindstone
