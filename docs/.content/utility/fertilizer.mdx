# Bio-Alchemical Fertilizer

The Fertilizer induces hyper-growth in plants maturing them fully. 

It even affects bone-meal resistant plants such as:
- [](@minecraft:sugarcane)
- [](@minecraft:cactus)
- [](@minecraft:nether_wart)
- [](@minecraft:chorus_plant)

Furthermore, it has the ability to:
- convert patches of [](@minecraft:dirt) into [](@minecraft:grass_block)
- force [](@minecraft:mycelium) to spread
- fertilize [](@biomancy:primal_bloom)
