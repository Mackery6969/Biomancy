# Primordial Tongue
Is an umbrella term that describes both the spoken and written language primarily used
by the primordial kin. The written language is often referred to as Primordial Glyph or Runes.

## Adoption by Biomancers
Occasionally Biomancers use the language to inscribe jokes, mantras, quotes and messages
on their equipment. A few of the public available equipment blueprints are known to include
quotes of sentences uttered by intoxicated Biomancers in the associations club room during
heated debates.
The inscriptions are mostly considered as a form of trolling or flexing of their glyph skills.
